FAQs

A few things to know.

What do I need to send?

A product link is enough to begin. Add brand guidelines, source footage or a specific brief when you send your request.

Can I use the video commercially?

Yes. Every delivered video includes commercial usage for your owned and paid social channels.

How is the expert involved?

The expert steers the AI agents at key checkpoints throughout production, bringing judgment and taste to the creative decisions and making sure the work meets the quality bar. They also review and approve the final video before delivery.

What counts as a revision?

One round of consolidated feedback covering copy, pacing, scene selection and the final call to action.
