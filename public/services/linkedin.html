FAQ

The fine print, plainly

Do you need my LinkedIn password?

No. Every post reaches you for approval and ships from your account through access you can revoke at any time. Your password stays yours.

Do I approve posts before they go out?

Yes. Nothing is published without your sign-off. Approve, edit, or bin any draft, and your edits teach the voice model what you sound like.

What counts as a relevant impression?

A view from the audience you name in the form: role, industry, seniority. We read the numbers from your own LinkedIn analytics and count those views, not raw reach.

Is there a minimum?

$99, once. That buys 10,000 relevant impressions. No retainer and no contract.

What if the impressions do not arrive?

The pack stays open until they do. A pack of 10,000 is a pack of 10,000; you never pay twice for the same reach.

How fast does it start?

The first post reaches you for approval within 48 hours of your two inputs.

Who actually does the work?

Agents research, draft, and match your voice. A human expert reviews everything before it reaches you and answers for what ships.
