FAQ

The fine print, plainly

Do you need my LinkedIn password?

Access and posting mechanics get defined at onboarding.

Do I approve posts before they go out?

Every post, comment, and engagement is reviewed by a human on our side before it ships. Your own approval flow is set up at onboarding.

What counts as my relevant audience?

The audience you define in onboarding. Impressions are counted against that, not raw views.

Is there a minimum budget?

Yes. One block of 10,000 impressions at $100 is the smallest we run. Below that there are not enough posts inside the window to tell whether the targeting is working, so you would be paying for a number instead of a result.

Who actually does the work?

Agents draft the posts, comments, and engagements. A human expert reviews everything and is accountable for what ships.
