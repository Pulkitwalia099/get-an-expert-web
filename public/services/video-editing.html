FAQ

The fine print, plainly

How much footage can I send?

There will be a limit, because ten hours of footage for a two minute edit is a different job to twenty minutes of it.

How long does it take?

Quoted with the price, before editing starts, so you know before you commit.

How many revisions do I get?

Working proposal is one round included, more priced separately.

What formats do I get back?

The ones you name in the intake form. A landscape cut and a vertical cut of the same video is a normal ask.

What about music rights?

If you have licensed tracks, send them. Otherwise we use music that is cleared for what you are doing.

Who actually edits it?

The agent does the selects, the first cut, and the captions. A human editor makes the calls on pacing and finish, and is accountable for what you receive.
