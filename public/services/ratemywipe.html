FAQ

The fine print, plainly

What is a wipe level?

A score from 1 to 5 for how well you work with an AI coding agent. It covers planning, reviewing, running tools, giving context, and verifying the result. The exact rubric is published with the source. Rubric TBD

Does my code leave my machine?

TBD Working intent is that the read happens where your sessions live and you choose what, if anything, gets sent.

Which transcripts does it read?

TBD Claude Code sessions first. Other agents depend on what they write to disk.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source and there is no paid tier of it. The paid services are the other six on the marketplace.

What do I do with the score?

The report names the skill packs and repos for your weakest areas. You install those and run it again to see the level move.

Who is behind it?

The same team that runs the marketplace. The source is public, so the scoring is checkable rather than something you take our word for.
