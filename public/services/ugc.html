FAQ

The fine print, plainly

Do you promise results?

No. We do not promise sales, cost per acquisition or return on ad spend, because those depend on your price, offer and landing page. What you are buying is the ad.

Why make four and send one?

Judgment is what you are paying for. Generation is cheap for us and metered for you, so buying credits means running everything you made, including the two that should have been binned.

Who actually makes it?

The agent researches, drafts and generates. A human picks the angle, directs the output and rejects most of it. Both are named above.

Which categories do you turn down?

Regulated health claims, fitness transformations, finance, and anything needing real lived experience on camera. AI actors underperform there and get rejected more often. We say so rather than take the money.

Do I need to give you ad account access?

No. Access is optional, and it only speeds up the what won report.

How fast is the first ad?

In your inbox within 24 hours of a complete brief.

Why is the first ad watermarked?

So you can judge it before anyone runs it. Say yes and we send the clean file, and the $29 comes off your first month.

Revisions and ownership?

Full usage rights once paid. One revision on the $29 ad. Up to two revisions per ad on a monthly pack.

Can I pick the actor?

Not on the $29 ad. On a monthly pack we agree the casting on the call.

What happens after I subscribe?

A call first. We take the full brief there, agree the objectives for the cycle, and set the casting. The three-field form is only for the $29 ad.
