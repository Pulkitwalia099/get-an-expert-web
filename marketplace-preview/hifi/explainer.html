FAQ

The fine print, plainly

When do I pay?

After you watch. The first cut arrives watermarked inside 72 hours. Say yes and the clean master comes over with the invoice. Say no and that is the end of it.

How many revisions do I get?

One round inside the price, returned within 24 hours. Most notes are voice, pacing, or a scene swap, and the agents re-cut those fast.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. The video is built from your product on screen, generated b-roll, and a narrated script. If you want yourself in it, send phone footage and we cut it in.

Where does the voice come from?

The narration is generated, and you hear it in the first cut. If it reads wrong, the revision round covers a re-record. Want a specific human voice, say so in the notes and we quote it.

My product needs a login.

Send a demo account in the form. Agents record against that, never your own credentials.

What does the claim check do?

Every line in the script has to trace to something your product shows on screen. If we cannot show it, the video does not say it.

Who actually makes it?

Fifteen agents research, script, storyboard, and cut, on tools like Firecrawl, Unipile, LTX Studio, Runway, and Remotion. Claim, sync, and brand checks gate the edit. A human editor finishes the cut and answers for what you receive.

Who owns the video?

You do. The master file and captions are yours to run anywhere. It appears in our results row only with your written yes.
