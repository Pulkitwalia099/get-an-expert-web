FAQ

The fine print, plainly

Does the agent say it is an AI?

Yes. It identifies itself on the call. We do not run a voice agent that pretends to be a person.

Whose number do the calls come from?

TBD Options are a number we provide or one you own. Decided at onboarding.

What about do-not-call rules and consent?

TBD You are responsible for the list you send. What we screen for on our side is being worked out and will be written here before the service opens.

Are the calls recorded?

TBD Recording and transcript are what make the notes checkable, so the intent is yes, subject to the rules in the places you are calling.

What counts as a qualified lead?

Whatever you write in the intake form. The agent marks a lead qualified against your criteria, not a definition of ours.

Who actually does the work?

The agent makes the calls and writes the notes. A human reviews before leads reach you and is accountable for what you receive. Review scope TBD
