Decision aid · before launch

Turn on the recording before the traffic arrives

The website promotion is going live soon. This is the one thing worth doing before it does, and the reason it cannot wait. Dashboards can come after. Recording cannot.

The one idea

Analytics is just a way of looking back at what was recorded. You can build a chart any time. But a chart can only show data from after the day recording was switched on. Anything that happened before then was never saved.

recording ON,
then promo
1,000 people visit, and every visit is saved to look at later kept
promo first,
recording later
1,000 people visit, and there is no record they were ever here gone

Same launch, same visitors. The only difference is the order. That is why recording goes in first.

What is already saved, and what is not

A fair amount is already being written to your database on every chat. The gaps are the three numbers you will most want on launch day.

Already recorded

  • ✓The conversations. Every chat message and how far it got.
  • ✓The searches. When someone's request turned into a real expert search.
  • ✓The leads. Every email someone left you, the money moment.
  • ✓Where they came from. The referring site is saved on each visit.

Not recorded yet

  • ✗Visits. A visit is only saved once someone types. People who look and leave are invisible.
  • ✗Where they quit. Saw the expert cards but did not ask for an intro? That drop is not saved.
  • ✗Real vs demo. Until the keys are set the site runs a scripted demo, and a demo looks identical to a real visit.

The plan, in the right order

Same pieces we discussed, resequenced so nothing is lost during the launch. Recording now, charts later.

Do now, before promo

Switch on the recording

  • Add PostHog to the chat. One tool that records visits, clicks, and where people drop, with all typing hidden.
  • Mark the drop-off steps so "saw the experts, then left" gets counted.
  • Flag demo visits so real traffic can be told apart from the scripted demo.

Result: from the first promoted visitor, everything is being saved. And PostHog has its own built-in charts, so you can already watch it without waiting for the custom dashboards.

Do later, once data flows

Build the custom dashboards

  • The private /admin page with the funnel, drop-off, conversion, and referrer charts on your own data.
  • First-party copies of the drop-off steps in your own database, if you want them beyond PostHog.

These only read data. They can be built next week or next month and will show everything recorded from launch day onward.

The one thing I need you to decide

Recording people's visits touches privacy law once real strangers arrive, especially from the EU or UK. Typing is always hidden either way. The question is only about the visit record itself. I will ask this next, outside the document.

Option A: quiet notice

A small line in the footer saying you use analytics, with a link. Fastest, common for early launches, lighter legal footing than a full banner.

Option B: consent banner

A cookie banner where people accept before recording starts. Safest legally, but it is a popup on your brand-new landing page, and refusers are not recorded.

PostHog
An analytics tool you drop into a website. It records what visitors do so you can see drop-off and replay real sessions later. Free at your volume.
Post-hoc analytics
Looking back at what happened after the fact. It only works if the events were being recorded at the time, which is the whole point of doing this first.
The funnel
The steps from visiting to leaving an email. "Drop-off" is where people fall out of those steps.
Demo mode
Until the API keys are set in the hosting settings, the chat serves scripted sample answers instead of the real AI. Useful for a preview, but its traffic must not be counted as real.
